GT:
BY BEING STUDIOUS OF COLOR THEY ARE STUDIOUS OF DIVISION AND WHILE THE CHIAROSCURIST DEVOTES HIMSELF TO THE REPRESENTATION OF DEGREES OF FORCE IN ONE THING UNSEPARATED LIGHT THE COLORISTS HAVE FOR THEIR FUNCTION THE ATTAINMENT OF BEAUTY BY ARRANGEMENT OF THE DIVISIONS OF LIGHT

Whisper(original):
By being studious of color they are studious of division, and while the curious purist devotes himself to the representation of degrees of force in one thing, unseparated light, the colorists have for their function the attainment of beauty by arrangement of the divisions of light.

Whisper(decoded):
By being studious of colour they are studious of division, and while the curious-curious devotes himself to the representation of degrees of force in one fanned, unsuppared light, the colourists have for their function, the attainment of beauty, by arrangement of the divisions of life.
